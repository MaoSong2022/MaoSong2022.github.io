## LFM 2.5

LFM2.5-24B-A2B [@LFM2.5-24B-A2B] 是 Liquid AI 发布的一个用于端侧设备的 MoE 模型

LFM2.5-24B-A2B 架构与 LFM2-24B-A2B 一致，

- 40 layers
- 64/4 experts
- hybrid attention (gated shorted convolutions and GQA)
- 131 context length

training

- pre-training tokens: 33T
- mid-training: 2T (32k context), 300 B (131K context)

post-training:

- sft
- specialization (SFT, RL)
- non-agentic MOPD
- agentic MOPD
- agentic RL
